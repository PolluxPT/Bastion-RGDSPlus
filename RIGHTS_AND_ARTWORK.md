# Rights and artwork boundary

## Included in the public package

Original integration source/scripts; custom FMOD-compatible managed audio adapter (not the proprietary FMOD SDK); licensed FNA/FNA3D/FAudio/Theorafile/NVorbis/Mono.Cecil components; and the official Linux ARM64 .NET runtime in release assets. Upstream licences/notices are retained. SDL2, EGL/GLES, OpenAL and system libraries come from the user's Stock OS and are not redistributed here.

## Excluded

Original or patched Bastion.exe, Bastion.bmp, Content packages/textures/audio/maps/fonts/scripts, soundtrack, store installers, APKs, DRM components, user saves, calibration data and gameplay logs. The original game assembly is patched only on the owner's machine/card and backed up locally. No proprietary game binary or binary delta is embedded in the patcher.

The supplied character/logo PNG has no established redistribution permission or artist provenance in this project. It is present only in the separately labelled PERSONAL install ZIP. Do not upload that ZIP or image as a public asset. The public release may generate an icon locally from a user's own optional Bastion.bmp; that generated icon remains game material and must not be repackaged as a project-owned asset.

## What the project licence does not grant

The MIT licence covers original integration code, not the game, artwork, trademarks or third-party components. A credit line does not by itself grant redistribution permission. Do not claim official endorsement or blanket copyright clearance. Local patching/interoperability must still be considered against the owner's game licence and applicable requirements. This document is a packaging boundary, not a legal opinion or guarantee.

Supergiant's website terms distinguish the game's EULA from website use and provide limited, conditional fansite permissions. Those permissions do not establish a general right to bundle this downloaded/fan-art icon in a release. Their video policy is also not a general game-asset licence. If you later want public artwork, establish the artist's permission and relevant game-rights permission first, retain the required notices, and review the applicable policy.
